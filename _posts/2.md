# Why is the Retail Trading Community Becoming So Big?

The retail trading community is becoming increasingly popular, especially in the financial sector. In fact, according to Bloomberg data, this community grew by 20% in the last year alone!

But why?

What makes trading so appealing to so many people?

How does this increase the risk of a potential financial crisis?

Investing can seem daunting at first glance, especially if you’re starting from scratch with no idea where to begin.

If you’re just getting started, or if you’ve been involved in trading for years but still feel like you’re not making the kind of progress you want, retail trading might be your answer.

This post will take you through some of the biggest factors that influence retail trading, and what you can do to trade safely and responsibly.


## The Benefits

The retail trading community has grown considerably over recent years, due to an increasing number of households having a single income or two incomes that are quite similar.

Successful retail traders generally trade in their free time – This means traders can earn extra income from home, which saves them money on transportation costs and enables them to spend more time with their families.

In addition, as most retail traders only trade during evenings and weekends, it does not interfere with work schedules. This means there are many opportunities for people who want to improve their financial situation but cannot leave their job to do so.

Moreover, although trading may seem complicated at first glance, it doesn’t take much time or effort to learn how to begin trading online. In fact, many people begin learning how to make trades while they are still in school!

Because of these reasons and more, trading has become a popular part-time business that can be extremely lucrative for those who have a knack for it!

## How We Got Here

To understand why retail trading communities are taking off, we have to go back in time to a simpler time...say 1994.

1994 was the year of The Internet.

This technology was still new and it was not widely adopted by most people or businesses.

There were no smartphones, Facebook, Instagram, or YouTube. The first graphical browser, Mosaic, was developed in 1993 and became widely used by 1994. Retail traders back then would use Bulletin Board Systems (BBS) to connect with other traders using a dial-up modem connection.

The rise of BBSs and message boards in 1994 led to discussion forums becoming widely used on websites such as Yahoo! Groups, Google Groups, Facebook, and Reddit today.

Many companies have set up retail trading chat rooms so that new and experienced investors can ask questions about stock trading, Forex trading, or crypto currencies without having to worry about scammers or getting into legal trouble for talking about stocks.

Retail traders on Facebook or Reddit can post their own personal trades. What's more, they can also learn from others in a very supportive community. A great example of a place where retail traders can share their experiences is StockTwits which has millions of users.

They have daily chats with popular financial experts such as Jim Cramer and regular discussions with individual traders on their platform. This enables them to share knowledge easily among themselves through social media sites like Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn, etc.

As technology gets smarter, so do retail traders.

These days it’s not enough to just trade and watch your profits grow. There are plenty of analysis tools available that can help beginners get a grasp of their portfolios.

Not to mention, a plethora of research tools!

These toolsets allow investors to find information about companies they are thinking of investing in. This allows successful retail traders and social traders to use discernment. Especially before buying their stocks or coins in cryptocurrencies.

Sites like Investopedia have trading forums where people can ask questions and give answers. This helps everyone learn more about trading stocks and make better trades themselves.
